# Jinku Guo

Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, China.

My book, [Beyond Apparent Paths: The Practice of Seeking Truth from Facts](../books/beyond-apparent-paths/README.md),
explores how order, life, consciousness and society take shape in a changing
world. Drawing on the sciences and Eastern and Western philosophy, it develops
the multi-level account of knowledge and action that provides the philosophical
and methodological perspective of this collection. A five-page English preview
presents the title page, the note to the reader and the complete chapter list.

My research programme develops mathematics, physics and computation from
minimal unbiased compositional change. The papers follow a common generating
source into arithmetic and expression histories, compact gauge and matter
structures, long-range response and certified finite computation.

The public collection brings together the book and the research manuscripts,
with individual archival identifiers for the papers. The Lean proof library
and V5 computational framework provide complementary implementations.
