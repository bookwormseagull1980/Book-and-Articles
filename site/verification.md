# Proofs, Calculations and Reproduction

The programme has two complementary public implementations. The Lean library
records formal declarations, hypotheses and theorem-to-paper mappings. V5
evaluates the physical response and retains its source dependencies, interval
enclosures and comparison data.

develops the Gaussian source, generated arithmetic, finite histories and
history-assembly dynamics. Its physical branch includes the generated
gauge-matter structures and the nonlinear Yang-Mills action, self-adjoint
Hessian and attained positive spectral gap.

## Reading a verification record

A formal declaration establishes its stated conclusion under its typed
hypotheses. A numerical certificate encloses a specified finite calculation
and its controlled remainder. A comparison statistic relates a specified
readout to measured data. The companion papers explain how these distinct
records support their mathematical and physical conclusions.

Each article page links the manuscript and any distributed computational
supplement. Cite the article for its result and the relevant software record
for a reproduction.
