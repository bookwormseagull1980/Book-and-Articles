# Research figures

The physical derivation, six-layer hierarchy and generated-action diagrams are
figures by Jinku Guo from the graphical introduction to the compact internal
space programme. The PNG reading copies and vector PDFs reproduce those
figures. Their dated author-year keys refer to the works linked and named in
the surrounding topic pages.

The common-source research map links the mathematical, physical and
computational branches presented in this collection. The three repository
sharing images summarize the corresponding public introductions.
