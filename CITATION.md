# Citing this collection

For the book's account of change, emergence, knowledge and action, cite:

Guo, Jinku. *Beyond Apparent Paths: The Practice of Seeking Truth from Facts*.
English book manuscript. [Book description and preview](books/beyond-apparent-paths/README.md).
Its [citation metadata](books/beyond-apparent-paths/metadata/CITATION.cff)
records the author, title, language and public introduction address.

Each article is a distinct work. Cite the article whose theorem, model or
calculation you use; its directory contains `metadata/CITATION.cff` and
`metadata/IDENTIFIERS.yml`. The [paper catalogue](README.md#papers) lists arXiv,
SSRN and Zenodo records in that order where available. Use the article's
preferred citation record. Zenodo concept DOIs identify archived works across
versions.

For formal declarations, cite the proof library and the companion manuscript:

Jinku Guo. *Lean Formalization of Arithmetic and Basic Physical Interfaces*.
[10.5281/zenodo.23254521](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23254521).

For executable physical calculations and their records, cite:

Jinku Guo. *Coarse-Graining Genesis Framework*.
[10.5281/zenodo.22067005](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22067005).

Repository revisions identify the source used in a reproduction. They are
separate from the concept DOI used in the publication citation. Rights for
each reading copy and supplement are stated in its `metadata/RIGHTS.md`.

The catalogue and research portal have no separate publication DOI.
