# Generated Arithmetic and Histories

Classical natural-number constructions determine values and their arithmetic.
This construction also retains how a value was produced. The distinction is
mathematical: two expressions can agree in value while carrying different
internal observables and different responses under assembly.

## From correlation to arithmetic

Minimal unbiased compositional change selects a rotation plane and a Gaussian
law. Local information and normalized total correlation determine the finite
window used in the construction. Preservation of constants selects the Neumann
closure within the separated homogeneous local boundary class. Its spectral
responses order an initially unlabelled family of modes.

After orientation, pointwise modulation and composition define the two native
operations. Modulation by the first positive mode gives successor. These
operations yield an ordered Peano semiring with unique prime factorization.
Winding degree then supplies its numerical certification. The modes retain
quadratic spectral energy and canonical scale and orientation transport.

## What an arithmetic value leaves unresolved

Ordered expressions retain brackets, operation labels and ordered inputs. Every
value has a finite expression fibre. A pure sum producing six has six unit
leaves, while an expression multiplying three by two has five. Their root
energy is the same, but the corresponding internal quadratic defects are
thirty and thirty-one. These are distinct data on a common value fibre.

Equal root-channel weights and independent subhistory assembly determine the
conditional history law used in the paper. Conditional expectation separates
value observables from internal history differences. Exact variance and entropy
identities quantify the information retained by this separation.

## Histories affect subsequent response

The complete history space carries a nonnegative self-adjoint trace-class
assembly operator. Its contraction evolution couples value coefficients to
history differences. An exact matrix element at value four gives a history
vector with zero initial value readout and nonzero readout after applying the
assembly operator. Eliminating the history-difference sector produces an exact
integral equation with memory.

This is the distinction from starting directly with the power maps of a circle.
The circle gives the arithmetic value representation; the preceding source
construction and the retained expression space supply response, internal
variation and history-dependent evolution.
