## Abstract

We present the philosophical basis and research programme of a generative framework for mathematics and physics. The central question concerns the origin of the objects, assumptions, and operations from which a theory begins. Logical consistency constrains their consequences but does not establish their correspondence with nature. Taking minimal unbiased change as the common starting point, the framework studies how relations close into ordered structures and how successive levels acquire their own objects and laws. The main argument develops this view from the discussion in Beyond Apparent Paths. Three appendices develop the physical, mathematical, and engineering pictures that guide the programme. A common source connects internal spectra, gauge and matter structure, a positive equilibrium Yang–Mills gap, collective gravity, and cosmic evolution. Retaining the formation of numbers and functions supplies history-dependent responses, a signed scale-energy approach to the Riemann hypothesis, and a study of efficient generation and verification. For the Navier–Stokes equations, finite evolutions with controlled residuals, stability, and spectral tails are completed into continuum solutions on certified intervals. The unifying aim is to trace the starting points of these problems to a common process of generation and to retain that process in their mathematical description.

## The starting-point question

A theory reasons from objects, operations and assumptions. What makes those
starting points appropriate to the world it describes? This article develops
the philosophical argument behind a common generative approach to mathematics
and physics. It follows the passage from change to persistent relations,
from relations to recognizable structures, and from structures to the distinct
objects and laws of successive levels.

The guiding idea is that a mathematical or physical description can retain how
its objects were formed. That information matters when two constructions have
the same immediate value but respond differently to later operations. It also
matters when finite observations are used to infer a continuum process.
Generation, observation and empirical correction consequently belong to one
account of how a theory acquires and tests its meaning.

## Three connected research directions

The physical appendix follows minimal compositional change through Gaussian
correlation and spectral structure to gauge and matter response, a positive
equilibrium Yang–Mills gap, collective gravity and cosmic evolution. Its concern
is the connection between these constructions and the physical picture that
organizes them.

The mathematical appendix explains how generated arithmetic and functions retain
expression histories, response operators and information about their formation.
It describes signed scale-energy estimates for the Riemann hypothesis and the
relation between efficient generation and verification. The complexity discussion
states the role of uniform certified routes and the condition connecting them
to the classical P-versus-NP problem.

The engineering appendix develops preparation, evolution and observation as parts
of a finite computation. Residuals, stability and spectral-tail estimates connect
finite Navier–Stokes trajectories to continuum solutions on certified intervals.
Adaptive models and reusable calculations extend this picture to practical
simulation.

## Place in the collection

This article gives a concise philosophical foundation and research programme
for the collection. [Beyond Apparent Paths: The Practice of Seeking Truth from
Facts](../../books/beyond-apparent-paths/README.md) develops the broader account
of change, order, life, consciousness and understanding. The technical papers
develop the corresponding mathematical constructions and physical models.

- [A Spectral Construction of Natural-Number Arithmetic](../a-spectral-construction-of-natural-number-arithmetic/README.md) develops arithmetic with retained expression histories.
- [Prime Decomposition and Operator Responses in Spectral Natural-Number Arithmetic](../prime-decomposition-and-operator-responses-in-spectral-natural-number-arithmetic/README.md) develops Hodge prime localization, occupation traces and signed responses.
- [The Spectrum of a Compact Internal Space I](../the-spectrum-of-a-compact-internal-space-i/README.md) and [II](../the-spectrum-of-a-compact-internal-space-ii/README.md) develop gauge–matter structure and quantitative couplings and mass scales.
- [A Spectral Criterion for Emergent Gravity](../a-spectral-criterion-for-emergent-gravity/README.md), [Coarse-Graining and Long-Range Response](../coarse-graining-and-long-range-response/README.md) and [Compact Internal Space III](../the-spectrum-of-a-compact-internal-space-iii/README.md) develop long-range response and cosmic dynamics.
- [Dynamics of Generated Spectra](../dynamics-of-generated-spectra-from-minimal-change/README.md) and [Certified Spectral Computation for Semilinear Evolution](../certified-spectral-computation-for-semilinear-evolution/README.md) develop generated functions, finite records and computational cost.
- The [Lean proof library](https://github.com/bookwormseagull1980/Lean-Formalization-of-Arithmetic-and-Basic-Physical-Interfaces) records formal statements and proof dependencies. The [computational framework](https://github.com/bookwormseagull1980/coarse-graining-genesis) supplies executable physical calculations and their certificates.
