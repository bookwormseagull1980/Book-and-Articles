## Abstract

From minimal unbiased compositional change we construct compact internal
geometry and gauge–matter representations, and prove a mass gap for the
generated Yang–Mills response.
On the least-dimensional nontrivial compact connected faithful irreducible orthogonal
carrier, independent composition and square-root scaling with unit covariance
fix the centred Gaussian source. Ladder operations and coherent comparisons
generate the round SU(2) process spectrum; complete centre-invariant
observations give RP³. Within complete continuations preserving
adjoints, exchange structures, source phase and reversal, three theorems
characterize the faithfully acting group
(SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1))/ℤ₆,
a sixteen-dimensional full matter module with a fifteen-dimensional charged
quotient, and an elementary scalar connector parametrized by one complex doublet.

The colour connection determines a complete spectral response with a unique
positive equilibrium at the source-calibrated radius. Its normalized radial
and common-frame coexact responses define the generated Yang–Mills mass
operator. The Gaussian equilibrium completion has a unique vacuum and an
attained positive gap. Its representation preserves the original connection
state and polynomial products and intertwines the update. The curvature
susceptibility detects the collective radial response.

The same source algebra generates a Lorentzian event carrier with a phase
clock. Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction gives free local fields.
For the renormalizable gauge–matter realization, causal perturbation theory
constructs the interacting net as a formal series at gauge-compatible finite
internal resolution.

## Scope

The mass-gap theorem concerns the generated Yang–Mills response operator and
its equilibrium Gaussian completion. The gauge and matter construction retains
the declared source operations and continuation rules. Lorentzian and
perturbative field realizations use the reconstruction and finite-resolution
conditions stated in the manuscript.
