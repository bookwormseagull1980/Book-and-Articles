## Abstract

We construct an internal gauge–matter response system and prove a positive
spectral gap for its generated Yang–Mills operator from minimal unbiased
compositional change. Independent composition and square-root scaling with
unit covariance fix a centred Gaussian law on the least-dimensional compact
connected faithful irreducible orthogonal carrier. Its ladder operations and
coherent comparisons generate the round SU(2) process spectrum. Complete
centre-invariant observations give RP³. The relative-frame continuation
together with the least-matter and elementary-connector continuations
preserves the generated adjoint and exchange structures and the source phase
and reversal. They determine (SU(3)× SU(2)× U(1))/ℤ₆ acting on a
sixteen-dimensional full matter module with a fifteen-dimensional charged
quotient. The elementary connector has one complex scalar doublet coordinate.
The colour connection supplies the collective intensity together with Casimir
feedback and the long-root drive. The complete spectral response has a unique
positive equilibrium at the source-calibrated IR radius L_src=√(2π)>L_c=√π.
Its normalized radial curvature and common-frame coexact response form the
Yang–Mills mass operator. Its equilibrium Gaussian response completion has a
unique vacuum and an attained positive gap. The original connection
polynomials retain their source state and products under the intertwined
equilibrium update. The curvature mean has a nonzero radial differential. The
first source corner also generates a Lorentz event carrier and a phase clock.
Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction gives free local fields. Native vertices
admit causal perturbative reconstruction at gauge-compatible finite internal
resolution. Anomaly cancellation gives the physical BRST net at every
perturbative order in the renormalizable gauge–matter realization. In the
quadratic field realization colour intensity has a radial pole and explicit
multiparticle continua.
