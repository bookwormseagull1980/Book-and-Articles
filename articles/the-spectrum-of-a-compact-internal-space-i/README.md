## Abstract

Minimal unbiased compositional change generates a source operation algebra
with compact internal and Lorentzian event realizations. On the
least-dimensional compact connected faithful irreducible orthogonal carrier,
independent composition and square-root scaling with unit covariance fix the
centred Gaussian law. Its ladder and coherent comparisons generate the round
SU(2) process spectrum; complete centre-invariant observations give RP³.
Within complete continuations preserving adjoints, exchange structures,
source phase and reversal, three theorems characterize relative frames,
least faithful matter and the elementary scalar connector. The faithfully
acting group is (SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1))/ℤ₆ acting on a sixteen-dimensional full
matter module with a fifteen-dimensional charged quotient. The elementary
connector has one complex scalar doublet coordinate.

The colour connection supplies a collective intensity, Casimir feedback and
the long-root drive. The complete spectral response has a unique positive
equilibrium at the source-calibrated IR radius L_src=√(2π)>L_c=√π. Its
normalized radial curvature and common-frame coexact response form the
generated Yang–Mills mass operator. Its equilibrium Gaussian response
completion has a unique vacuum and an attained positive gap. The original
connection polynomials embed with their source state and ordinary products
intact, and the representation intertwines the equilibrium update. Explicit
Hermite weights describe their curvature correlations, and the joint
curvature susceptibility detects the collective radial response.

A source phase clock calibrates timelike translation on the event carrier.
Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction gives free local fields. At
gauge-compatible finite internal resolution, causal perturbation theory
realizes the native vertices as a local net. Its physical net is the quantum
BRST cohomology, with anomaly cancellation ensuring the Ward identities at
every order in the renormalizable gauge–matter realization. In the quadratic
realization, colour intensity has a radial pole and explicit multiparticle
continua.
