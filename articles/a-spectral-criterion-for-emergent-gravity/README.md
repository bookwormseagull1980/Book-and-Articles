# A Spectral Criterion for Emergent Gravity

Author: [Jinku Guo](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-6600-6171)

Work type: Research article

Repository snapshot: [21 October 2026](https://github.com/bookwormseagull1980/Book-and-Articles/tree/a-spectral-criterion-for-emergent-gravity-2026-10-21/articles/a-spectral-criterion-for-emergent-gravity)

## Abstract

We develop a source-resolved spectral criterion for long-range tensor exchange
coupled to conserved stress-tensor sources. Exact channel elimination carries the
propagation kernel, physical source, readout and direct response together, so that
the observable residue is independent of the chosen intermediate variables. For an
isolated critical subspace, common-space operator estimates control the residue at
the displaced root of a finite approximation. A positive Källén–Lehmann
representation then identifies the residue with the weight of a
zero-invariant-mass atom, including the case in which the atom lies at the
endpoint of continuous support. We prove separately that the null-shell part of a
complete positive, Lorentz-covariant conserved stress-tensor measure is
longitudinal and therefore has no transverse-traceless atomic weight. In a compact
spectral realisation, the internal tensor vertices are carried into an external
two-helicity response. For localised stationary matter, the conserved energy
vertex gives a mixed residue proportional to the two total energies, and spatial
resolvent estimates determine the leading potential and force together with their
finite-size corrections. After the collective tensor is normalised through its
matter vertex, a single dimensional calibration fixes the overall Planck scale,
while the spectral residue fixes the corresponding dimensionless exchange
strength. Together, these results specify the positivity, helicity, universality
and nonlinear consistency conditions under which the collective tensor admits an
Einstein–matter effective description.

## Keywords

Emergent gravity; Stress-tensor correlation functions; Källén–Lehmann representation; Massless spectral atoms; Long-range tensor exchange; Schur–Feshbach reduction; Analytic Fredholm theory; Matter coupling.

## Scope

The criterion concerns the complete response from a conserved source to a tensor
observation. The null-shell theorem applies to the full covariant stress-tensor
measure. The reduced two-helicity response has its own state space and matching
conditions. Its gravitational interpretation is tied to the stated matter-coupling
and nonlinear consistency requirements.
