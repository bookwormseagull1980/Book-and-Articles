# A Spectral Criterion for Emergent Gravity

Author: [Jinku Guo](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-6600-6171)

## Abstract

We give a source-resolved spectral criterion for long-range tensor exchange
between conserved matter sources. Exact channel elimination preserves the
propagation kernel together with the source and readout maps and the direct
response. Common-space estimates control the residue at the displaced critical
root of a finite approximation. A positive Källén–Lehmann representation
identifies this residue with a zero-invariant-mass atom even at a continuum
endpoint. For a complete positive Lorentz-covariant conserved stress tensor we
classify the null-shell measure and prove that its TT atomic weight vanishes.
A reduced compact spectral realisation instead carries internal tensor
vertices into an external two-helicity response. Conservation reduces the
leading vertex of localised stationary matter to its total energy. The mixed
residue then factorises into the two energies and a common propagation
coefficient. Spatial resolvent estimates determine the potential and force
with finite-size corrections. Normalisation through the matter vertex leaves
one dimensional calibration for the overall Planck scale. The spectral residue
fixes the dimensionless exchange strength. The resulting response admits an
Einstein–matter effective description under the stated positivity and helicity
assumptions together with universal soft coupling and local nonlinear
consistency.

## Keywords

Källén–Lehmann representation; Stress-tensor correlators; Analytic Fredholm
theory; Long-range tensor exchange; Emergent gravity.

## Scope

The criterion concerns the complete response from a conserved source to a
tensor observation. The null-shell theorem applies to the full covariant
stress-tensor measure. The reduced two-helicity response has its own state
space and matching conditions. Its gravitational interpretation uses the
stated matter-coupling and nonlinear consistency requirements.
