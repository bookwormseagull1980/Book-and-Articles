## Abstract

Minimal unbiased compositional change generates the Gaussian source underlying
the compact internal construction. Its gauge and matter content together with
its scalar sector determine effective couplings and mass scales through common
spectral responses and kinetic normalizations. Joint screening selects three
matter copies. Shell support and position retention select the finite response
level R=5. The transverse-traceless endpoint at this level has a unique
positive root. Eliminating shared transfer parameters gives a relation among
six geometric fermion masses and correlates the electron and electroweak
scales. It also gives two cosmological ratios independent of the common
neutrino mass normalization. Finite one-loop matching implements the generated
Fermi condition together with the top and Higgs conditions in a common action
and gives a Born top width of 1.53445 GeV. A local nonlinear source–potential
system and an auxiliary-radius model realize source feedback. The latter has
positive reduced propagation and a matter-phase clock on a specified finite
cosmological interval. The fixed DESI DR2 distance comparison gives χ²=29.5088
for thirteen components and p=0.00554. This identifies tension in the adopted
cosmological mapping. Spectral and quadrature bounds control the
finite-response calculation. Mass-scheme matching and thermal approximations
enter the physical comparisons separately.
